# Branding review: US Ham Exam

## Screening date

2026-09-19

## Name reviewed

**US Ham Exam**

Current descriptor: **FCC Amateur Radio License Study**

## Findings

An informal web and product search found no obvious product using **US Ham Exam** as its exact name. Search results were generic references to US ham exams or distinct amateur-radio products, rather than an apparent conflicting brand.

The name is descriptive and region-specific. That makes the app's FCC/US scope clear, but also means the name should not be treated as a strong exclusive trademark without professional advice.

- Exact web searches for `"US Ham Exam"`, `"US Ham Exam" trademark`, and `"US Ham Exam" app`.
- Variants and nearby terms including US ham exam, US ham test, and amateur-radio exam products.

The USPTO guidance recommends searching exact wording, expanded combinations, alternative spellings, and related goods/services. A comprehensive clearance search may require additional sources and professional advice.

## Decision

No branding change is required at this time. Continue using **US Ham Exam — FCC Amateur Radio License Study** as the public-facing identity.

This review is an informal screening, not a trademark opinion or legal clearance. Before registering or commercially investing in the name, repeat the search in the USPTO database and consider a qualified trademark professional.
