Start here: this is a US FCC Amateur Radio exam study app. Three license classes: Technician (the usual starting point), General (broader privileges after Technician), and Extra (the highest class).

Portable operation: a radio, an antenna, a notebook, and a view. No fixed station required.

What people do with it

  • Parks on the Air (POTA): set up a portable station in a park for an afternoon and make contacts with other operators.
  • Hiking, camping, and mobile radio: carry a handheld radio on the trail, or install one in a vehicle to stay in touch on the road.
  • Satellites and the ISS: small satellites — and even astronauts aboard the International Space Station — relay amateur contacts with modest, affordable gear.
  • Digital modes and experimentation: send text, images, or data over radio, or build and modify your own equipment and antennas.
  • Emergency and public-service communication: trained operators support disaster response and community events when ordinary networks are down or overloaded.
  • Home stations, clubs, and mentors: build a station at home and join a local club, where experienced operators ("elmers") help newcomers learn by doing and make new friends along the way.
