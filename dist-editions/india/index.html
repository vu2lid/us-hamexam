About this practice app


This app is an independently authored practice tool for the Indian Amateur Station Operator's Certificate (ASOC), Restricted grade. It is not an official examination, not an official question pool, and not a replica of any official paper.




New here?





The current practice bank covers only a small, deliberately limited set of syllabus topics — see "Current practice topics" below for exactly what is and is not included. This is an early practice slice, not complete syllabus coverage.







Getting started


    

  • One practice question is shown at a time; the correct answer is hidden during the selected recall period.
    • 

  • Tap Menu (☰) to open Settings, where you can change the Reveal after recall timer, change the Theme, and reach Mock Exam, Help & About, and Reset progress.
    • 

  • Use Previous and Next at the bottom of the screen to move through questions, and Reveal Now to show the practice answer immediately.
    • 








Current practice topics


This practice bank currently covers a small, documented subset of the Restricted syllabus: elementary electricity and magnetism, basic AC theory, and the ITU phonetic alphabet/Q-codes. It does NOT yet cover semiconductors, receivers, transmitters, propagation, aerials, frequency measurement, the amateur rules themselves, emission designations, frequency allocations, or distress/urgency procedure. Do not treat this as a complete study resource for the official examination.
